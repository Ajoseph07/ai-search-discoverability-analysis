# AI Search Optimization Audit

## Website

**Company:** Soundcore

**Product:** Liberty 5

**Website:** https://www.soundcore.com

---

## Objective

Evaluate a consumer electronics product page to identify opportunities for improving Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO).

## SEO Audit

### Question

Can search engines clearly understand what this page is about?

### Findings

- The page title clearly identifies the product and brand.
- The hero section immediately explains the product's primary purpose as AI recording earbuds.
- Content is organized into descriptive feature sections, making it easier for both users and search engines to understand the page topic.

### Observation

The page provides a clear understanding of the product and its key features through descriptive headings and structured content.

## AEO Audit

### Question

Does the product page clearly answer the questions a potential customer is likely to ask?

### Findings

- The page explains the product's primary purpose immediately.
- Product features are grouped into clear sections with concise descriptions.
- Technical specifications are presented in a structured format.
- The page does not include a dedicated Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) section addressing common customer questions.

### Observation

The page provides strong product information, but adding an FAQ section could help users find answers more quickly and improve answer engine optimization.

## GEO Audit

### Question

Does the product page provide structured and descriptive content that AI assistants can easily summarize when answering user questions?

### Findings

- The page includes clear descriptions of the product's primary features and benefits.
- Product specifications are organized into structured sections.
- Feature explanations provide sufficient context for AI systems to summarize the product.
- The page could further improve AI readability by including concise question-and-answer content and product comparison information.

### Observation

The product page contains detailed information that supports AI-generated summaries. However, adding FAQs and comparison content could improve its usefulness for generative AI search experiences.

## Recommendations

### 1. Improve SEO

Add more descriptive product copy targeting common search terms such as "AI recording earbuds," "wireless earbuds for meetings," and "noise-canceling earbuds." This can help improve relevance for users searching for these topics.

### 2. Strengthen AEO

Add a Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) section that answers common customer questions about battery life, AI recording, compatibility, and noise cancellation. This makes information easier for users and answer engines to access.

### 3. Enhance GEO

Create comparison content (for example, "Liberty 5 vs AirPods Pro" or "Liberty 5 vs Sony WF-1000XM5") and concise feature summaries that AI assistants can more easily reference when generating product recommendations.
