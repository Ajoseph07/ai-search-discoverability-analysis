#AI Search Discoverability Analysis for Consumer Electronics: AEO, GEO & Competitive Content Strategy

## Overview

This project explores how leading consumer electronics brands can improve their visibility across traditional search engines and AI-powered search experiences.

Using the wireless earbuds market as a case study, the analysis evaluates search intent, competitor content strategies, Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) to identify opportunities for improving AI search discoverability.

---

## Business Problem

Consumers increasingly rely on AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity to research products before making purchasing decisions.

This project analyzes how well consumer electronics brands appear within these AI-powered experiences and develops strategic recommendations for improving visibility, content quality, and discoverability.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze consumer search intent
- Evaluate competitor content strategies
- Assess Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)
- Assess Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
- Identify content gaps
- Develop strategic recommendations for improving AI search discoverability
